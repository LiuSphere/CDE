## Code And Primary Basis

Restored GAVDACLearner._actor_loss computes
L_base = -mean(Q_mixed) + .001*mean(raw_actions**2).
The raw_actions are bounded normalized tanh outputs; the controller maps
them affinely into each physical Box. Their zero corresponds to generator
half-range and zero battery dispatch for the frozen microgrid Boxes.
This is a learned-policy auxiliary objective, NOT an environment reward,
emissioncost,cyclecost,physical constraint or dispatch-cost formula.
No claim that its presence is a bug or the diagnosed cause of cost gaps.

Primary sources read live2026-10-24 before preregistration:

- Silver et al., Deterministic Policy Gradient Algorithms, ICML2014,
  Section3.2 Theorem1/Eq9 and Section4.2/Eq14-18: policy improvement
  follows an action-value gradient, with an approximate off-policy form.
  Section4.3 warns an approximate critic need not give an ascent direction.
  This motivates testing a pure-Q surrogate; it does not establish
  convergence or economic benefit in this recurrent actor-conditioned mixer.
  https://proceedings.mlr.press/v32/silver14.pdf
- OpenAI Spinning Up official PyTorch DDPG compute_loss_pi returns the
  negative mean of ac.q(o,ac.pi(o)), without this action-L2 term.
  This is an official reference objective, not an isolated regularizer
  ablation and not a microgrid result or a fullDDPG reproduction here.
  Its other assumptions,including uniform action limits,are NOT adopted.
  https://raw.githubusercontent.com/openai/spinningup/master/spinup/algos/pytorch/ddpg/ddpg.py
- Original FACMAC official continuous learner train contains a negative-Q
  mean plus1e-3 action-square mean. Thus the existing penalty has a genuine
  upstream implementation precedent. H09 deliberately ablates it; it is
  NOT a correction that makes the code more faithful to original FACMAC.
  That source's physical-action semantics need not match current normalized
  raw_actions; do not claim the entire objectives/algorithms are identical.
  https://raw.githubusercontent.com/oxwhirl/facmac/main/src/learners/facmac_learner.py

SilverPDF and SpinningUpraw snapshots/SHA are retained under
results/c2opt21/h09_primary_sources_a. FACMAC content was read through
the live primary web source; its local raw capture is explicitly absent:
raw IncompleteRead and GitHubAPI403 rate-limit failures are preserved in
h09_probe_transport_failure_a.json/b.json and both partial source dirs.
No invented sourcebytes or assertion removal. Local algebra was separated
from network capture. No production or formal incident/interactions.

Conjecture: the fixed normalized-action centering pressure can bias the
Actor away from economically useful dispatch when it competes with the
learned mixed-Q gradient. Removing only that pressure may meet the frozen
three-seed economics. This is a new single ablation, NOT a claim that the
penalty dominates real training, harms performance, or promises3%.
The penalty value is bounded by.001 for bounded rawactions and may be
economically irrelevant. Synthetic gradient magnitude is not real-training
importance. Removal can worsen saturation/critic-exploitation/variance,
and can harm dispatch even though physical clipping remains unchanged.
