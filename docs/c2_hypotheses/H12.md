## Primary Motivation And Risks

Primary sources read live2026-10-28:
Mnih et al.,Nature518(2015),doi10.1038/nature14236,Methods PDFpage7,
describes linear error influence outside the unit region:
https://storage.googleapis.com/deepmind-media/dqn/DQNNaturePaper.pdf .
Official PyTorch HuberLoss defines the quadratic/linear loss:
https://docs.pytorch.org/docs/stable/generated/torch.nn.HuberLoss.html .
Patterson,Liao,White,Robust Losses for Learning Value Functions,
arXiv2205.08464 discusses the distinction between common semi-gradient
Huber regression and robust Bellman-error optimization:
https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.08464 .

These motivate a bounded-influence TD experiment,not a DQN reproduction,
robust-saddlepoint algorithm,diagnosed harmful-outlier bug,convergence or
microgrid-economic guarantee. MSE and Huber can target different values
under asymmetric stochastic returns;Huber may suppress rare costly events.
Actor2may interact adversely with it. A conventional unit threshold in
current reward units is not a tuned optimum. H04's seed0regression is
negative evidence retained explicitly. No claim of a local raw paper
snapshot unless a separately verified download receipt exists.
