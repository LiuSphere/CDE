## Review And Primary Basis

Current policy maps local information through fc1, ReLU, GRUCell, fc2, tanh.
Both timestep and full-history paths lack feature normalization. This is a
code fact, not a diagnosis of unstable activations, saturation or a bug.
The bounded review considered Actor feature normalization, prioritized
replay and Q-normalized Actor loss. Choose only the first: it has a
well-defined local transformation and per-example statistics compatible
with online recurrence and uneven learner shards. Do not implement a queue
of the other ideas.

Primary sources read live 2026-10-28:

- Ba, Kiros, Hinton, Layer Normalization, arXiv1607.06450v1, sections3,
  3.1 and6: https://arxiv.org/html/1607.06450v1 .
  Per-example hidden normalization before activation motivates the placement.
  Its normalized recurrent gates are NOT the proposed unchanged GRUCell.
- Official locked-version PyTorch2.11 LayerNorm documentation:
  https://docs.pytorch.org/docs/2.11/generated/torch.nn.LayerNorm.html .
  Last-dimension biased variance, eps1e-5, learned affine ones/zeros and
  input statistics in train/eval define the mechanical contract.
- Schaul et al., Prioritized Experience Replay, arXiv1511.05952v4:
  https://arxiv.org/abs/1511.05952 .
  DQN/Atari results do not specify this recurrent shared replay's episode
  priorities, importance weighting or refresh rules. Defer, do not bundle.
- Fujimoto and Gu, A Minimalist Approach to Offline RL, arXiv2106.06860,
  and author TD3_BC.py:
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.06860 and
  https://raw.githubusercontent.com/sfujim/TD3_BC/main/TD3_BC.py .
  Q scaling there balances an offline behavior-cloning objective. It does
  not directly justify rescaling this online Actor's action-L2 objective.

Conjecture: normalizing the Actor's learned input features can make the
inherited policy optimization more useful economically at the same physical
budget. It is a testable representation hypothesis, not a LayerNorm-GRU
reproduction, diagnosed local cause, convergence theorem or 3% promise.
No additional held-out inference or economic pilot is used to select it.

Negative evidence: H06 normalized both Critic preactivations on the root,
but mean saving was only 0.8320% and the round was rejected. That is a
different intervention, not evidence that normalization is generally good
or generally useless. H09's removal of Actor action-L2 worsened its mean
and is not inherited. H12's small retained improvement does not support a
specific normalization mechanism.
