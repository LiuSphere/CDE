## Code And Primary Basis

Restored GAVDACCritic builds fc1/fc2/fc3 with ordinary nn.Linear.
Only fc3 maps hidden48 to one local Q; it has no custom initialization.
GAVDACLearner constructs this Critic, deep-copies its target, then
constructs the original GraphDec mixer and optimizers. This is a code
observation, not a diagnosed initialization bug or economic failure cause.

Primary sources checked live2026-10-24:

- Lillicrap et al., Continuous Control With Deep Reinforcement Learning,
  ICLR2016, supplementarySection7 EXPERIMENT DETAILS, page11:
  finalweights AND biases of actor/critic use uniform[-.003,.003]
  in low-dimensional experiments, motivated by small initial outputs.
  This is a whole-DDPG setting, not an isolated small-init ablation or
  evidence of standalone microgrid benefit. Actor initialization and other
  DDPG architecture/normalization/hyperparameters are NOT adopted here.
  https://arxiv.org/pdf/1509.02971
- OpenAI Baselines official baselines/ddpg/models.py Critic.__call__:
  final dense kernel uses uniform[-.003,.003]. Its unspecified dense bias
  initializer is NOT evidence that it randomizes bias in that range;
  H08weight+bias choice follows the paper, not exactBaselines reproduction.
  https://raw.githubusercontent.com/openai/baselines/master/baselines/ddpg/models.py
- PyTorch official v2.11.0 nn.Linear reset_parameters source, matching
  installed2.11.0+cu128, initializes weights/biases in
  [-1/sqrt(fan_in),1/sqrt(fan_in)]. Locked dependencies unchanged;
  CPUfloat32 execution remains mandatory despite the installedbuildsuffix.
  https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pytorch/pytorch/v2.11.0/torch/nn/modules/linear.py

Conjecture: reducing only the initial local Critic output-layer amplitude
can improve early bootstrapping/Actor learning enough to meet frozen
economics. Not a claim that baseline Q is too large, unstable or
overestimated. GraphDec has its own nonlinear mixing and additive V(state);
leaving them unchanged means totalmixedQ is NOT guaranteed nearzero or a
scaled copy of baseline. No3%promise.

Risks: much smaller initial Critic action gradients may let the unchanged
Actor regularizer dominate early learning; small initial estimates can
underfit nonzero returns, slow hidden-layer learning, alter the transient
target bias or worsen seedvariance/cost. Adam/epsilon/gradientclip can make
parameter scaling unlike an exact learning-rate rescale. No causal benefit
is inferred from the paper's fullalgorithm or from the mechanical probe.
