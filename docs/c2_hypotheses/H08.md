## Code And Primary Basis

Restored GAVDACCritic builds fc1/fc2/fc3 with ordinary nn.Linear.
Only fc3 maps hidden48 to one local Q; it has no custom initialization.
GAVDACLearner constructs this Critic, deep-copies its target, then

observation, not a diagnosed initialization bug or economic failure cause.

Conjecture: reducing only the initial local Critic output-layer amplitude
can improve early bootstrapping/Actor learning enough to meet frozen
economics. Not a claim that baseline Q is too large, unstable or
overestimated. GraphDec has its own nonlinear mixing and additive V(state);
leaving them unchanged means totalmixedQ is NOT guaranteed nearzero or a
scaled copy of baseline. No3%promise.

Risks: much smaller initial Critic action gradients may let the unchanged
Actor regularizer dominate early learning; small initial estimates can
underfit nonzero returns, slow hidden-layer learning, alter the transient
target bias or worsen seedvariance/cost. Adam/epsilon/gradientclip can make
parameter scaling unlike an exact learning-rate rescale. No causal benefit
is inferred from the paper's fullalgorithm or from the mechanical probe.
