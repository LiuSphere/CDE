## Conjecture And Intervention

The Actor can influence mixed Q through both its actions and its hidden-state
attention context. Holding the latter fixed during Actor optimization may
remove a non-action gradient route and improve dispatch economics.

Only change: `actor_mixer_context_grad:false` in `gavdac_c2_h01`.
Detach hidden context in the Actor-loss mixer call, retain action gradients.
Default main behavior remains true. No simultaneous width, learning-rate,
EA schedule, reward, data or budget changes.

C3 and common conditions are frozen. Validation-only RL Actor selection,
three fresh seeds0/1/2,6Mjoint interactions each including EA, same original
host assignment and continuous182-day evaluation remain unchanged.
